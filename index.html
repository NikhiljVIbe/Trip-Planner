Step 4 · Island hop

Pick your flight KL to Langkawi

It's a short 1-hour hop. With kids, comfort > cost — pick a daytime slot with assigned seats so you can stay together.

Best with kids: Malaysia Airlines from KLIA — full service, free snacks, free baby food on request, friendlier crew with infants. Firefly from Subang (SZB) is also great if your KL hotel is on the west side.

Step 5 · Beach stay

Pick your Langkawi resort

Beach resorts with calm shallow water, kids' clubs, and easy access to Indian / multi-cuisine food.

Heads up: Accor group has a small footprint in Langkawi. Most flagship family resorts here are luxury independents (St. Regis, Westin, The Andaman, Four Seasons, Ritz-Carlton).
