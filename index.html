🌦️ Weather in June – July

Tropical and humid. KL stays drier (southwest monsoon mostly bypasses it). Langkawi gets afternoon showers from southwest monsoon — sea is warm but plan beach time in mornings.

Plan around it: Outdoor in morning, malls / aquariums / pool 2 – 5 PM, dinner stroll after rain.

👶 Travelling with toddlers

Hard-won wisdom from Indian families who've done this trip.

🍼
Stroller you can fold one-handed
KLIA + KLIA2 walks are long. A one-handed fold lets you carry a sleepy kid + push gear simultaneously.
🦟
Mosquito repellent (Odomos)
Especially in Langkawi rainforest + at dusk. Long sleeves help too.
☀️
Heat strategy: split the day
One outdoor activity in the morning, AC pool / aquarium / mall 2 – 5 PM, evening stroll. Don't try to do more.
🍛
Indian food everywhere
Saravanaa Bhavan, Sangeetha, Annalakshmi. Brickfields = Little India. Most hotel breakfasts have dosa + paratha.
🚗
Grab app, not taxis
Cheaper, English driver, child seats on request. Download + add card before you land.
💧
Bottled water + ORS
Tap water is technically safe but stick to bottled to be safe with kids. Carry ORS sachets for any tummy upset.
🧴
SPF 50+ for everyone
Apply 20 min before going out, reapply every 2 hr at the beach. Use mineral sunscreen for kids' faces.
📱
eSIM or local SIM
Airalo eSIM ~ ₹500 for 5 GB, or Maxis prepaid at airport (~RM 30 for 30 GB / 7 days).

🎒 Packing checklist for kids

5 nights · light suitcase. Most "forgot" items are easily bought in KL.

👕 Clothes
  • 5–7 cotton t-shirts
  • 3–4 shorts
  • 2 swimsuits + swim diapers
  • 1 light rain jacket
  • Sun hat + kid sunglasses
  • Sandals (Crocs) + sneakers
  • 2 pajama sets
💊 Health & toiletries
  • SPF 50+ kids sunscreen
  • Odomos mosquito roll-on
  • Anti-allergy + ORS sachets
  • Thermometer + paracetamol
  • Diapers + wipes (1 pack)
  • Hand sanitizer + soap bar
  • Bandaids + antiseptic
🎧 Tech & comfort
  • Universal travel adapter (Type G)
  • Electric kettle (for warm milk)
  • Stroller + carrier
  • Kids' tablet + headphones
  • Favorite snacks + ready-meals
  • Refillable water bottle
  • Comfort toy / blanket
